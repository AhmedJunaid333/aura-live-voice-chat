# DATA RETENTION POLICY

## 1. Configurable Retention Schedules Matrix

| Data Classification Category | Data Entity Type | Standard Retention Period | Legal / Financial Requirement | Deletion / Anonymization Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Profile** | Username, Email, Phone, Bio | Account Lifetime + 30 Days | User Consent & Service Delivery | Anonymization / Soft Delete |
| **Financial Ledger** | Wallet & Diamond Transactions | 7 Years | Tax & Anti-Money Laundering (AML) | Retained in Ledger |
| **Audit Logs** | Immutable System Audit Records | 5 Years | Regulatory Security Compliance | Write-Only Log Storage |
| **Live Stream Records**| Audio Lounge Metadata & Chat | 90 Days | Platform Moderation & Safety | Automated Cleanup Daemon |
| **Security Events** | Login Attempt Records | 180 Days | Fraud Detection & Security Audit | Automated Log Rotation |
