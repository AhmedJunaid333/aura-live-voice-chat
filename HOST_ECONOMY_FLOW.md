## 1. Virtual Gifting & Diamond Conversion Mechanics

```
Viewer Coin Wallet (Coins Deduction)
             ↓
Virtual Gift Transfer (Lucky Gift / Standard Gift)

             ↓
Monthly Streamer Target Evaluation (Target Hours e.g. 50.0h)
             ↓
Target Bonus Payout Calculation ($150.00 USD)
             ↓
Financial Ledger Audit Log Record (`prisma.walletTransaction`)
```
