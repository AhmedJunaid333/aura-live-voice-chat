# Privacy Policy for Aura Live Voice Room

**Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## 1. Information Collection
Aura Live Voice Room collects minimal account information (nickname, profile avatar, country code, device ID) to provide voice broadcasting services.

## 2. Audio Processing
Real-time audio streams are processed solely for room broadcasting and AI safety moderation. We do not store raw audio streams unless flagged for violation.

## 3. Data Protection
All data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.3. User account data is stored securely in encrypted databases.
