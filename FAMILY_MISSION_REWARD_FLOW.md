# FAMILY MISSION & REWARD FLOW SPECIFICATION

## 1. Monthly Guild Mission & Reward Lifecycle

```
Guild Mission Activation (Monthly Goal e.g. 50,000 XP)
             ↓
Members Contribute via Live Hours, Gifts & Engagement
             ↓
Mission Completion Validated by Express Backend
             ↓
Guild Level Transition & Reward Bonus Pool Credited
             ↓
Audit Log Record (`FAMILY_XP_ADDED`) & Socket.IO Notification
```
